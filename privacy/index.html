Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

Captix ("the App", "we", "us") is developed by Elyosoft. This policy explains what data the App handles, why, and your choices. By using Captix you agree to this policy.

We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email, or contact information.

1. Data We Process

a. Your videos and audio

To generate captions, the App extracts the audio from the video you select and sends that audio to our speech-to-text provider for transcription. The resulting caption text may also be sent to an AI provider to clean up or translate it when you use those features.

  • Your video file itself stays on your device. Captioning and export are performed locally; only the audio (for transcription) and caption text (for optional cleanup/translation) are transmitted.
  • We do not store your videos, audio, or transcripts on our own servers.
  • These third-party providers process this content only to return a result to the App. See "Third-Party Services" below.

b. Anonymous usage analytics

To understand how the App is used and improve it, we collect anonymous, aggregate analytics:

  • A randomly generated, anonymous identifier created on your device (not your name, email, or advertising ID).
  • App events such as app opens and video exports.
  • Approximate region, language, and time zone derived from your device settings.

This data is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you across other apps or websites. It is processed by our analytics provider.

c. Photo library access

The App requests permission to access your photo library so you can select videos to caption and save captioned videos back to your library. This access is used only for those purposes and is handled on your device.

d. Purchases and subscriptions

Captix offers an auto-renewable subscription (Captix Pro). All in-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. Subscription title, length, and price are shown in the App and on the App Store before purchase, and subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. See our Terms of Use (EULA) for full subscription terms. Apple's handling of purchases is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or contact list.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers (IDFA) or device-level IDs.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your data for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

3. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following providers to deliver its features. Your audio or text may be processed by them as described above:

  • ElevenLabs (speech-to-text transcription) — https://elevenlabs.io/privacy
  • OpenAI (optional caption cleanup and translation) — https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Apple (in-app purchases) — https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Our analytics provider (anonymous usage metrics)

These providers process data under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

4. Data Retention

  • We do not retain your videos, audio, or transcripts on our servers.
  • Anonymous analytics are retained in aggregate to monitor and improve the App.
  • Third-party providers retain data according to their own policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Captix is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will address it.

6. Your Rights

Because we do not maintain accounts or store personally identifying data, we generally hold no personal information tied to you. Depending on your region (e.g. GDPR, CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. To make a request, contact us at the address below.

7. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for data transmitted between the App and our providers. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect data in transit.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
