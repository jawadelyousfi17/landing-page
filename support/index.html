Frequently asked questions

How do I add captions to my video?

Import or record a video, and Captix automatically transcribes the audio word by word. Pick a style, tweak any words, then export — all in minutes.

Which languages does Captix support?

Captix transcribes and translates captions into 30+ languages, with first-class Arabic and full right-to-left (RTL) support.

Can I translate my captions?

Yes. Use the Translate tool in the editor to convert your captions into dozens of languages.

What export quality is available?

Captix burns captions directly into your video and exports crisp footage up to 4K, ready to share on Reels, TikTok, Shorts, and more.

Can I customize the caption style?

Absolutely. Control colors, outlines, shadows, backgrounds, size, spacing, alignment, and fonts — including premium Arabic typefaces — plus animated karaoke and word-by-word reveals.

Can I add background music?

Yes. Add and mix music tracks right inside the editor before you export.

The transcription got a word wrong. Can I fix it?

Yes. Tap any word on the timeline to edit the text or fine-tune its timing.
